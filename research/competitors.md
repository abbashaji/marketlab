# Competitors and price map

Sources: public product and pricing pages only. Prices are list prices as understood at writing time and must be re-checked on the linked page before use. 'complaints' are common public themes, not quotes.

## H-001

- **Claude Code** https://www.anthropic.com/claude-code . terminal coding agent, multiple sessions possible . price: $20/mo . complaints: usage limits hit quickly in heavy sessions, parallel sessions share the same quota
- **Cursor** https://cursor.com . AI editor with background agents . price: $20/mo . complaints: request limits on paid tier, agents can conflict when editing the same files
- **GitHub Copilot** https://github.com/features/copilot . IDE assistant and coding agent . price: $10/mo . complaints: premium request caps, weaker multi-step repo-wide work
- **Windsurf** https://windsurf.com . agentic AI IDE . price: $15/mo . complaints: credit-based limits, no coordination between parallel sessions
- **OpenAI Codex** https://openai.com/codex . cloud and CLI coding agent . price: $20/mo . complaints: bundled with ChatGPT plan limits, tasks run isolated from each other
- **Aider** https://aider.chat . open-source terminal pair programmer . price: $0/mo . complaints: you pay model API costs, manual merge of parallel work
- **Cline** https://cline.bot . open-source VS Code agent . price: $0/mo . complaints: token costs via own API key, single-session oriented
- **OpenHands** https://github.com/All-Hands-AI/OpenHands . open-source autonomous dev agent . price: $0/mo . complaints: self-hosting setup effort, API costs
- **Google Jules** https://jules.google . async coding agent on GitHub repos . price: $0/mo . complaints: daily task caps on free tier, limited steering mid-task
- **Replit Agent** https://replit.com . browser IDE with agent . price: $25/mo . complaints: usage-based charges add up, less suited to existing large repos
- **Manual workaround: git worktrees plus several chat tabs** https://git-scm.com/docs/git-worktree . copy-paste context between sessions . price: $0/mo . complaints: manual task splitting, merge conflicts, context re-explained each time

## H-002

- **Upwork freelancers** https://www.upwork.com . hire a developer per task . price: no public price . complaints: slow hiring, variable quality, cost per hour
- **Fiverr developers** https://www.fiverr.com . fixed-price gigs for code tasks . price: no public price . complaints: scoped gigs rarely fit a repo-specific task, quality varies
- **Toptal** https://www.toptal.com . vetted freelance engineers . price: no public price . complaints: expensive, onboarding delay
- **Codementor** https://www.codementor.io . paid mentoring and code help sessions . price: no public price . complaints: hourly rates, help is advice not delivered work
- **GitHub Copilot coding agent** https://github.com/features/copilot . assign an issue, get a pull request . price: $10/mo . complaints: premium request limits, mixed PR quality on complex issues
- **Google Jules** https://jules.google . submit a repo task and get a PR . price: $0/mo . complaints: free-tier task caps, limited control
- **OpenAI Codex** https://openai.com/codex . cloud agent works on a connected repo . price: $20/mo . complaints: needs paid ChatGPT plan, usage limits
- **Devin** https://devin.ai . autonomous software engineer agent . price: no public price . complaints: cost for heavy usage, results need review
- **Factory** https://factory.ai . enterprise coding droids . price: no public price . complaints: sales-led access, aimed at teams not individuals
- **Algora** https://algora.io . open-source bounties for tasks . price: no public price . complaints: slow, uncertain who picks up the task
- **GitHub good first issue / community help** https://github.com/topics/good-first-issue . ask maintainers or volunteers . price: $0/mo . complaints: no guarantee of response, depends on goodwill
- **Manual workaround: do it yourself with a chat assistant** https://claude.ai . paste files and iterate by hand . price: $0/mo . complaints: hits free usage limits, tedious copy-paste loop

## H-003

- **GitHub template repositories** https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/creating-and-managing-repositories/creating-a-template-repository . start new repos from a template . price: $0/mo . complaints: generic scaffolds, no AI workflow guidance
- **Cookiecutter** https://github.com/cookiecutter/cookiecutter . project scaffolding from templates . price: $0/mo . complaints: setup is code-centric, nothing for AI chat workflows
- **Yeoman** https://yeoman.io . scaffolding generators . price: $0/mo . complaints: aging ecosystem, no agent or prompt content
- **Vercel Templates** https://vercel.com/templates . starter apps . price: $0/mo . complaints: tied to Vercel stack, app starters not process templates
- **Awesome ChatGPT Prompts** https://github.com/f/awesome-chatgpt-prompts . community prompt list . price: $0/mo . complaints: unstructured, not repo or coding specific
- **Cursor Directory** https://cursor.directory . community rules for Cursor . price: $0/mo . complaints: tool-specific, quality uneven
- **AGENTS.md** https://agents.md . open format for agent instructions . price: $0/mo . complaints: a format only, few ready-made working templates
- **Anthropic Prompt Library** https://docs.anthropic.com/en/prompt-library/library . sample prompts . price: $0/mo . complaints: general prompts, not multi-session repo workflows
- **OpenAI Cookbook** https://cookbook.openai.com . code examples and guides . price: $0/mo . complaints: developer examples, not ready-to-use templates
- **Notion templates** https://www.notion.com/templates . planning and workflow templates . price: $0/mo . complaints: not connected to code or repos
- **PromptBase** https://promptbase.com . marketplace for paid prompts . price: no public price . complaints: paid per prompt, quality varies
- **Gumroad template packs** https://gumroad.com . creators sell template bundles . price: no public price . complaints: paywalled, hard to judge before buying
